## 📌 Abstract
A profound structural recalibration currently defines the global financial architecture. Legacy credit instruments indigenous to centralized finance (TradFi) are actively migrating into decentralized finance (DeFi) ecosystems, driven by the tokenization of Real-World Assets (RWAs). 

This analytical paper deconstructs the financial and infrastructural architecture of the **Apyx protocol** natively deployed on the **Solana** network. It investigates the novel "Digital Credit" asset class, analyzing its collateralization via publicly traded preferred equities (**STRC** and **SATA**), cash buffers, and deterministic periodic yields designed to resolve the yield stagnation bottleneck in digital fiat assets.
